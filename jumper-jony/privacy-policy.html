Jumper Jony – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Jumper Jony ("the game") is a free single-player game made by Rabeya Noor. This policy explains what data is used when you play.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free because it shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device type and app interaction data (for example, which ads were shown and clicked). This data is processed by Google according to How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Rewarded ads are always optional: they are only shown when you tap a "Watch ad" button.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best times, coins, weapons, skins, power-ups and rescued queens) is saved only on your own device. You can erase it from the game's INFO screen ("Reset progress") or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load ads.
  • Advertising ID – used by AdMob for ads.
  • Vibration – short haptic feedback during gameplay.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
